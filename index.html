1. Introduction

Welcome to Funny Sound Effects. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use Funny Sound Effects.

Funny Sound Effects is designed to work primarily offline. We do not require you to create an account and we do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

💾

3. App Data

The app may store certain preferences and usage data locally on your device, such as favorite sounds, played counts, and recently played information. This information is stored locally and is used only to provide app functionality.

🎵

4. Audio Files

The sound effects included in the app are used to provide the core functionality of the application. Audio files are played locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

🔗

Funny Sound Effects does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account or personal information, it is designed to minimize the collection of user data.

🔄

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
